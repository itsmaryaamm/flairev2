# Demo video script (under 3 minutes, no music, no third-party logos)

Record the desktop dashboard in dark mode, 1080p. Keep the PayPal sandbox tab ready in a second window.

0:00 Problem (talk over the Flaire site hero)
"AI agents can now spend money. Nobody is in control of that. Flaire is the finance department for AI employees, and it pays through PayPal."

0:20 Under the limit
Show AI workforce, Marketing AI card (PayPal Sandbox, limit). In ChatGPT or the API: "Buy a Canva plan, SAR 375." Flaire approves. Open Spending: the transaction shows "PayPal is processing it", then settled. Show the PayPal panel (USD 100.00 at 3.75).
"Flaire checked the budget, limit and category first. Only then did PayPal get the payout."

1:00 Show the money
Switch to the PayPal sandbox Personal account: the payment arrived.

1:20 Over the limit, human approves
Request SAR 900 (over the employee's approval threshold). It lands in Approvals. Press Approve, enter your password, and Flaire sends you to PayPal. Log in with the sandbox buyer account, confirm, and you return to Flaire where it shows paid.
"The AI cannot approve itself. A person approves twice: in Flaire, and in PayPal."

2:00 Blocked and failed
Request a Gift card: stopped, PayPal never called. Show the kill switch turning all spending off.
Mention: if PayPal fails a payout, the budget is given back automatically.

2:30 Why it matters
"Companies want AI that can buy things, but only inside rules. Flaire is that rule layer, built on PayPal Payouts, with a Gemini assistant and a ChatGPT and Claude connector. Try it at flaire-nine.vercel.app."
